RASPU - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

RASPU is a Windows desktop application designed to display live streaming chats and help streamers monitor and moderate their communities.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the connected platforms and enabled features, RASPU may process account identifiers, usernames, profile information, chat messages, live stream events, moderation information, and authentication credentials or tokens.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to connect authorized accounts, display live chat, provide notifications, and perform supported moderation actions.

4. Third-Party Platforms

RASPU interacts with third-party streaming services, including TikTok, Twitch, and Kick. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

5. Data Storage and Security

RASPU is designed as a desktop application. Authentication credentials and personal information are handled according to the security measures implemented in the application.

6. Data Sharing

Data may be transmitted to connected platforms when necessary to provide authorized functionality. Additional data sharing practices, if introduced, will be disclosed in this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may disconnect their platform accounts. Data retention and deletion depend on the application's implemented functionality. Users may request information about their data or its deletion by contacting the application operator.

8. User Rights

Where applicable, users may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, or object to processing under data protection laws, including the GDPR.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to RASPU and its integrations. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, data protection questions, or requests regarding personal information, contact the RASPU application operator at zorgiw8@gmail.com.
