RASPU - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

RASPU is a Windows desktop application designed to display live streaming chats and help streamers monitor and moderate their communities.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the connected platforms and enabled features, RASPU may process account identifiers, usernames, profile information, chat messages, live stream events, moderation information, and authentication credentials or tokens.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to connect authorized accounts, display live chat, provide notifications, and perform supported moderation actions.

4. Third-Party Platforms

RASPU interacts with third-party streaming services, including TikTok, Twitch, and Kick. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

5. Data Storage and Security

RASPU is designed as a desktop application. Its storage and security practices depend on the implemented features. Authentication credentials and personal information must be protected against unauthorized access.

6. Data Sharing

Data may be transmitted to connected platforms when necessary to provide authorized functionality. Additional data sharing practices, if introduced, will be disclosed in this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may disconnect their platform accounts. Local data deletion and retention depend on the application's implemented functionality. Users may contact the application operator regarding privacy and deletion requests.

8. User Rights

Users may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of their personal data under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR where applicable.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to RASPU and its integrations.

10. Contact

Privacy inquiries may be directed to the RASPU application operator using the official support contact published alongside this policy.
